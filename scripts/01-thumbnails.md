# Episode 1: Thumbnail Prompts
**Title:** Every Ghanaian SHS Stereotype Explained · 1280×720 (16:9)

## Rules learned from Explain Chale's data
- Their **top 3 thumbnails** (137K, 82K and 33K views) all show **cartoon characters + an emotion + a conflict**. Their **bottom 5** show objects only.
- Keep the brand layout: **one huge word, a gold "EXPLAINED" tag, at most 3 words in total**, readable at phone size.
- **Kofa appears in every thumbnail** so viewers start recognising the brand.
- Generate the image **without text**, then add the text in Canva or Photoshop: Baloo 2 ExtraBold, white with a thick black outline, and the "EXPLAINED" tag in kente gold `#FFC933` on forest green `#0B5D30`.
- Leave the **bottom-right corner empty**, because the video length covers it.

**Style suffix (add to every prompt):**
> flat 2D vector cartoon, thick black outlines, bold saturated colors, simple rounded characters with oversized heads and very exaggerated facial expressions, high contrast, clean uncluttered composition, empty space on the left third for text, YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, no text

## Thumbnail 1: "The Lineup" (identity, closest to the 137K winner)
**Subject:** four cartoon students standing in a row in four different-coloured school uniforms:
1. a posh boy in sunglasses holding car keys
2. a nerdy boy with big glasses, equations floating above his head
3. a neat girl with a tiny halo, holding a rulebook
4. a big boy flexing beside a talking drum

Kofa (the golden Sankofa bird in a kente scarf) pops in from the right edge.

**Expressions:** each student with an exaggerated stereotype face (smug, calculating, angelic, aggressive grin). Kofa with eyes wide, beak open, shocked.

**Background:** forest-green background with a faint Ghana map outline and soft sunburst rays behind the students.

**Text:** **"SHS"** (huge, white) + **"STEREOTYPES"** + a gold **"EXPLAINED"** tag, on the left third.

**Emotion trigger:** *identity / "which one am I?"* Every viewer looks for their own school's type and clicks to see whether theirs is roasted.

**Prompt:**
> Four cartoon Ghanaian high school students standing in a row in four different colored school uniforms: a posh boy in sunglasses twirling car keys, a nerdy boy with huge glasses and math equations floating above his head, a prim schoolgirl with a small golden halo holding a thick rulebook, a big muscular boy flexing next to a talking drum; each with an exaggerated stereotype expression (smug, calculating, angelic, aggressive grin); a chubby golden cartoon bird with a kente scarf and a small egg in its beak leaning in from the right edge looking shocked with eyes wide; forest green background with faint Ghana map outline and soft sunburst rays; characters on the right two-thirds + STYLE SUFFIX

## Thumbnail 2: "Chop Box Crime" (nostalgia + conflict, like the "SOAP?!" thumbnail)
**Subject:** a dark boarding-school dormitory at night. A sneaky student is frozen mid-theft, one hand inside an open wooden chop box, holding a chocolate-malt tin. The box's owner sits up in a bunk bed, caught in a torch beam. Kofa is in the corner with its wings over its beak.

**Expressions:** the thief is sweating with a guilty "uh-oh" grin. The owner is furious, with eyes popping and veins showing. Kofa is gasping.

**Background:** bunk beds with mosquito nets in deep blue night tones, a strong yellow torch beam lighting the scene.

**Text:** **"MY CHOP BOX?!"** (white, slightly tilted) + a gold **"SHS EXPLAINED"** tag.

**Emotion trigger:** *nostalgia + outrage + humour.* Every ex-boarder has lived this ("someone stole my provisions"), and the conflict creates instant tension.

**Prompt:**
> Dark cartoon boarding school dormitory at night with metal bunk beds and mosquito nets, a sneaky teenage student frozen mid-theft with one hand inside an open wooden chop box holding a chocolate malt drink tin, sweating with a guilty grin, the owner sitting up in the top bunk furious with popping eyes, a bright yellow flashlight beam cutting across the scene, a chubby golden cartoon bird with a kente scarf in the corner gasping with wings over its beak, deep blue night palette with strong yellow highlight, characters on the right two-thirds + STYLE SUFFIX

## Thumbnail 3: "Santa's School?!" (curiosity gap)
**Subject:** a serious Ghanaian schoolboy in a crisp uniform and school tie, standing stiffly in front of an old colonial school building, wearing a red Santa hat. Kofa beside him, also in a Santa hat, holding a bell.

**Expressions:** the schoolboy is deadpan, completely serious, saluting. Kofa has one eyebrow raised and looks confused.

**Background:** a sunny Cape Coast scene with a white castle, palm trees and blue sky, plus a few cartoon snowflakes falling (the absurd contrast).

**Text:** **"SANTA'S SCHOOL?!"** + a gold **"SHS EXPLAINED"** tag.

**Emotion trigger:** *curiosity / "that can't be real."* An unexpected pairing of Santa and a Ghanaian school forces a click to resolve it. This works best for viewers outside Ghana and the diaspora.

**Prompt:**
> A serious cartoon Ghanaian schoolboy in a crisp school uniform and striped tie standing stiffly at attention and saluting, completely deadpan, wearing a red Santa Claus hat, in front of an old white colonial-era school building; beside him a chubby golden cartoon bird with a kente scarf also wearing a Santa hat, holding a small bell, one eyebrow raised in confusion; sunny tropical coastal background with a white castle, palm trees and bright blue sky, a few cartoon snowflakes falling for absurd contrast, characters on the right two-thirds + STYLE SUFFIX
