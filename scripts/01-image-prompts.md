# Episode 1: Image Prompts
**Every Ghanaian SHS Stereotype Explained** · 109 prompts, one for about every 2 sentences of the script (16:9, 1920×1080)

---

## How to use this file
1. Each prompt is **Scene + Camera + Lighting + Mood + the style block named in its Style field**. Paste the full style text (A, B, C or D below) at the end of every prompt.
2. **Add text, labels and titles in your editor (CapCut or Canva), not in the AI prompt.** Image generators misspell words. The prompts leave empty space where a label goes.
3. **Real people** (Rawlings, Kufuor, Kofi Annan, Mahama) are shown as **silhouettes only**, never as a likeness.
4. **Brands and crests:** use "chocolate malt drink tin" instead of the Milo logo, and plain shields in school colours instead of real crests. Add real crests yourself only where you have the right to use them.
5. **Kofa** must stay consistent. Upload `channel-kit/mascot-kofa.png` as a character or style reference (Midjourney `--cref`, Leonardo Character Reference, or an image prompt in ChatGPT or Ideogram).

## Competitor visual analysis (Explain Chale)

| Element | What they do | What we do better |
|---|---|---|
| Characters | Stick figures: round brown heads, dot eyes, thin stick limbs, trapezoid clothes, dark-brown outlines | **Rounded cartoon humans** with bean-shaped bodies, real hands, and **expressive eyebrows and mouths**, plus Kofa as the star |
| Faces | Almost always the same small smile, so there's little emotion | Big, exaggerated reactions (shock, pride, side-eye, crying) for comedy and retention |
| Backgrounds | (1) Cream sunburst with faint doodles for concepts; (2) flat pastel colours for lists; (3) simple flat locations such as an Accra beach with landmarks, or the Adomi Bridge | The same three types, in **our palette** (forest green, kente gold, red, cream) and with more detailed Ghanaian locations |
| Text | Cream rounded labels, brown outline, yellow pill sub-labels, a top headline bar with one coloured keyword | Cream labels with **black** outlines, **gold** pills, a **green** headline bar with the keyword in **red**, in the Baloo 2 font |
| Camera | Almost always static, straight-on, wide | **Varied:** close-ups for punchlines, low angles for pride, top-down for objects, zoom-punch on jokes |
| Lighting | Flat daylight | Flat daylight for most scenes, **sepia** for history, **muted cool** for the serious section |
| Transitions | Fading out old elements, curved yellow arrows | Keep the arrows, and add Kofa bumping into things as a transition gag |
| Branding | Logo watermark bottom right | Kofa watermark bottom right |
| Real locations | Named landmarks with signage (Kejetia Market, Black Star Gate, Jamestown lighthouse, Adomi Bridge), drawn with deeper perspective and cast shadows | Keep it: every school or town gets a recognisable location shot. Add the name sign in editing |
| List labels | Tilted red-on-cream tags: "#2 SWEET TALKERS" | Tilted **gold-on-green** tags: "#1 THE CHOP BOX", "STEREOTYPE #2: THEY DON'T SPEAK TWI" |
| Verdict cards | Two stacked cards: "GOOD BUSINESS ✓" / "…OR SWINDLERS ✗" | Use the same idea for every roast-then-defend beat: **"STEREOTYPE ✗" / "THE REAL STORY ✓"** cards |

## On-screen text system (add in editing)
- **Chapter title bar:** green rounded bar across the top, white Baloo 2 ExtraBold, keyword in kente gold
- **List tag:** tilted gold pill with a black outline, "#1", "#2"… (used in Achimota, PRESEC and the Chapter 7 countdown)
- **Name label:** cream rounded box with a black outline, plus a small red pill under it for the place or meaning ("BLEOO" / "SLOWLY")
- **Verdict cards:** red "✗ STEREOTYPE" card, then green "✓ THE REAL STORY" card sliding in below. Use these at P020, P034, P044, P065 and P075.
- **Location sign:** a big sign at the top of establishing shots ("CAPE COAST", "KUMASI", "ODUMASE-KROBO", "TAMALE")

## Style blocks (paste one at the end of every prompt)

**STYLE A (main):**
> flat 2D vector cartoon illustration, clean thick black outlines, simple rounded cartoon characters with oversized round heads, bean-shaped bodies, warm brown skin tones, big expressive eyes and eyebrows, soft flat shading, minimal detail, color palette of forest green #0B5D30, kente gold #FFC933, Ghana red #D7263D, cream #FFF3DA, sky blue accents, soft drop shadows under characters, YouTube animated explainer style, 16:9, no text

**STYLE B (history / flashback):**
> same flat 2D vector cartoon style, sepia and faded cream color grade, subtle paper grain texture, soft vignette, old-photograph feel, thick black outlines, 16:9, no text

**STYLE C (concept / infographic):**
> flat 2D vector cartoon, plain cream background with soft radial sunburst rays and faint doodle shapes (stars, circles, adinkra symbols), large empty space for labels, centered composition, thick black outlines, palette forest green, kente gold, Ghana red, 16:9, no text

**STYLE D (serious section):**
> flat 2D vector cartoon, muted desaturated palette with cool blue-grey tones and one warm gold highlight, soft evening light, calmer composition, thick black outlines, 16:9, no text

**KOFA (add whenever Kofa appears):**
> Kofa: a chubby golden cartoon bird, head turned back over its shoulder, small cream egg in its beak, green-gold-red kente scarf, red crest, big round eyes, one raised eyebrow
