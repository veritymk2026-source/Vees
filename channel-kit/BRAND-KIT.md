## 2. Mascot: **Kofa** the Sankofa bird

The file is `mascot-kofa.png`, and the source is `src/mascot.svg`.

**Design:** a chubby golden bird with its body facing forward and its **head turned backward**, holding a **golden egg** in its beak, like the Adinkra Sankofa symbol. It wears a kente scarf (gold, green, red, black), a red crest, big expressive eyes and one raised eyebrow.

**Personality:**
- Curious, cheeky and proudly Ghanaian, the friend who knows the backstory to everything.
- A little dramatic: *"Ei!"* at every shocking fact.
- Roasts everyone equally, then defends everyone (the roast-then-defend formula).

**Running gags** (these build attachment and turn viewers into subscribers, which Explain Chale lacks):
- Kofa always walks forward while looking backward, so it keeps bumping into things: trotros, market stalls, aunties.
- The golden egg is Kofa's "egg of knowledge". Kofa protects it dramatically, and when a fact is really big, the egg glows.
- Kofa is "300 years old" whenever it's convenient, and claims to have personally witnessed history.

**Catchphrases:**
- Opening line: **"Ei! Let's go back and fetch it."**
- Before the twist: **"Hold my egg…"**
- Sign-off: **"Now you know where it came from. Go back, fetch it, come again."**

## 8. Playlists (create them before the first upload)
1. **Every Ghanaian ___ Explained**: the identity and stereotype list videos (the flagship series)
2. **How Ghana Really Works**: systems (trotro, MoMo, markets, housing), told through people
3. **Ghana History, Fetched**: origin stories and history
4. **Ghana vs The World**: the Nigeria rivalry, the diaspora, "burgers"
5. **Kofa Shorts**: 30–60 second clips with a hook in the first second

## 9. Upload schedule
- **2 long videos a week** (Tuesday and Friday, 6 pm GMT). That's evening in Ghana and the UK, and midday on the US East Coast for the diaspora.
- **1 Short every day**: 3–5 Shorts cut from each long video, each opening with its own hook.
- Pin a comment on every video with an identity question ("What's YOUR ___?").

## 10. Channel trailer (45 seconds, for non-subscribers)
1. **0–5s:** Kofa walks backward into a trotro. *"Ei! Sorry, sorry. I'm a Sankofa bird. I always look back."*
2. **5–20s:** A rapid montage of questions: *"Why is our water in a bag? Why does your name say what day you were born? Why does Kasoa have the reputation it has?"*
3. **20–35s:** *"Every week, I go back and fetch the story behind something you see every day: who started it, who makes the money, and the secrets nobody tells you."*
4. **35–45s:** *"Ghana, explained. Subscribe, and let's go back and fetch it."* The egg glows, then the logo appears.
